Connecting Every Drop to Those Who Need It.

BloodBridge helps hospitals, donors and blood banks connect quickly during critical blood requirements.

How It Works

Four simple steps connect every request to the right source of blood.

1

Register

Donors, hospitals and blood banks create a profile with the details that matter for matching.

2

Request

Hospitals submit an emergency blood request with blood group, units and urgency.

3

Match

The system checks inventory and donor availability to surface compatible potential matches.

4

Save a Life

Blood banks confirm and dispatch, while everyone tracks the request through to completion.

About BloodBridge

BloodBridge is a Software Engineering academic project: a centralized platform where donors, hospitals, blood banks and administrators coordinate emergency blood matching, request tracking and inventory management.

Blood Compatibility (Informational)

The compatibility logic shown in this prototype is simplified demo logic built for academic demonstration. It must not be used for real medical decisions.
